The Problem We Solve
Over 50% of household waste in India is organic and ends up in landfills producing methane. BioBot converts kitchen scraps into rich compost right at home, automatically, with zero effort.
🔬
Engineering Behind It
Built using ESP32, DHT22 sensor, MQ-135 gas sensor, DS3231 RTC, OLED display, DC gear motor, 6V 5W solar panel and TP4056 charger inside a durable ABS weather-sealed body.
🤖
AI-Powered Intelligence
Integrated with Gemini AI through a standalone Telegram chatbot. Users can ask anything about composting and receive smart, expert answers anytime — even when the device is offline.

How It Works
Simple. Smart. Automatic.
BioBot handles everything — you add scraps and harvest compost 60 days later.
1
Add Kitchen Scraps Daily
Open the round twist-lock input hatch and drop vegetable peels, fruit waste and coffee grounds into the 20L basket. Cover with cocopeat or dry paper.
🥦 Greens + Browns
2
3 Sensors Monitor 24/7
DHT22 tracks temperature and humidity. MQ-135 monitors gas and ammonia. All readings update every 2 seconds on OLED and Blynk app.
📡 Continuous Sensing
3
Motor Mixes Every 8 Hours
The horizontal paddle inside the basket spins automatically for 60 seconds every 8 hours. Also trigger manually from the Blynk app.
⚙️ Auto Aeration
4
Monitor on Blynk App
Open Blynk on your phone to see live gauges, graphs and health status. Receive push notifications if any reading goes out of range.
📱 Live Dashboard
5
Ask BioBot AI on Telegram
Message the BioBot Telegram chatbot anytime for composting advice. Powered by Gemini AI and runs 24/7 on cloud independently.
🤖 AI Answers
6
Harvest at Day 60
Pull the bottom drawer for finished compost. Drain leachate from the side tap — dilute 1:10 with water as liquid fertiliser.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about BioBot.
How many sensors does BioBot use? +
BioBot uses a 3-factor monitoring system. The DHT22 sensor measures both temperature and humidity simultaneously. The MQ-135 sensor detects gas levels including ammonia and CO2. Together these three parameters give a complete picture of compost health.
Does BioBot need grid electricity? +
No. BioBot runs on a 6V 5W solar panel with dual 18650 batteries providing over 12 hours of backup. Zero electricity cost.
How do I monitor readings remotely? +
Download the free Blynk IoT app on your phone. Once connected to WiFi, BioBot sends live readings every 2 seconds to colour-coded gauges on the app. You also receive push notifications for any alert conditions.
What is the AI chatbot and how is it different? +
The BioBot Telegram chatbot is powered by Google Gemini AI and runs independently on cloud 24/7 — even when the physical device is offline. It answers any composting question, explains stages, troubleshoots problems and provides expert advice.
How long does composting take? +
A full cycle takes 45 to 60 days through 4 stages: Mesophilic (0-7 days), Thermophilic (7-30 days), Cooling (30-45 days) and Curing (45-60 days).
Will it smell bad? +
No. BioBot has a weather-sealed ABS body with rubber gaskets and an activated charcoal and neem filter above the input hatch. The MQ-135 sensor alerts you instantly if gas levels spike with exact steps to fix it.
Does the OLED work without WiFi? +
Yes. The OLED display works completely independently of WiFi. It always shows live temperature, humidity, gas level, day count and health status. Only the Blynk app and notifications require WiFi.
How much does BioBot cost to build? +
The complete BioBot prototype costs approximately Rs.8,500 including all electronics, mechanical parts and materials. Running cost is near zero thanks to solar power.
