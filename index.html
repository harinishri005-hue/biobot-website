FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions.

No. BioBot runs entirely on solar power. The 5.5V panel charges an 18650 battery via TP4056. A 1N5819 Schottky diode prevents reverse drain at night. All sensors, motor, display, and WiFi run from stored solar energy — zero electricity bill.
BioBot uses three active sensors: DHT22 for temperature and humidity (two readings, one sensor), MQ-135 for ammonia and CO₂ gas detection, and a capacitive moisture probe for pile wetness. The DS3231 RTC tracks the exact calendar day count stored in NVS flash memory so it survives power cuts.
The ESP32 pushes sensor data to Blynk every 5 minutes via WiFi (safe for the free plan). You see live gauges for temperature, humidity, gas, moisture, day count, and health status. Alerts are push notifications that fire when any sensor goes out of range. You can also trigger the motor manually via the Mix button (V6, Push mode).
A full cycle takes 45–60 days through four stages: Mesophilic (Days 1–7) where microbes establish, Thermophilic (Days 8–20) with peak heat 55–65°C and fastest decomposition, Cooling (Days 21–45) as temperature drops, and Curing (Days 46–60) for maturation. BioBot's DS3231 RTC tracks exact days and Blynk notifies you when compost is ready.
Yes, fully. Without WiFi, BioBot continues reading all sensors every 30 seconds, auto-mixing every 8 hours, counting days accurately via the DS3231 RTC, and displaying live readings across 6 OLED pages. The day count is saved in NVS flash and survives any power cut. Only Blynk monitoring and the Telegram AI bot require internet.
The Telegram bot runs independently on Railway cloud — it is completely separate from the ESP32 hardware. It uses Qwen 3.6 27B AI to answer composting questions: what to add, how to fix smells, when to harvest, interpreting sensor readings. It works 24/7 even when BioBot is powered off.
BioBot uses WiFiMulti to register both your home WiFi and mobile hotspot. It automatically connects to whichever is available and strongest. If both fail, it runs in offline mode and retries every 30 seconds. Turn on your hotspot before powering BioBot and keep your phone screen on during boot for reliable connection.
