Core Systems

Compost Needs.

Eight integrated systems work together to make composting effortless, automatic, and intelligent.

☀️
Solar Powered
5.5V 2.2W solar panel charges 18650 battery via TP4056 module. Runs all sensors, motor, and WiFi 24/7 with zero electricity cost — even at night.
Off-Grid

DHT22 — Temp + Humidity
One sensor provides both temperature (±0.5°C accuracy) and humidity readings simultaneously. Monitors the ideal 40–65°C thermophilic range and 50–70% humidity window.
Dual Sensing
💨
MQ-135 Gas Sensor
Detects ammonia, CO₂, and odour gas spikes in real time. Alerts via Blynk when gas exceeds 500 ADC — preventing anaerobic conditions before they cause bad smells.
Real-Time
🌊
Capacitive Moisture Probe
Capacitive soil moisture sensor measures compost pile wetness accurately without corrosion. Maps raw ADC to 0–100% moisture scale for easy interpretation.
Calibrated
🔆
LDR Lid Detection
Light-dependent resistor detects if the lid is open or closed. Blynk sends an alert if lid is left open — preventing insects and moisture loss from the bin.
Smart Alert
⚙️
Auto Bidirectional Mixing
DC gear motor with horizontal paddle runs every 8 hours — 5 seconds forward then 5 seconds backward for thorough aeration. Trigger manually via Blynk button anytime.
Auto-Aeration

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Does BioBot need electricity from the grid? +
No. BioBot runs entirely on solar power. The 5.5V 2.2W solar panel charges an 18650 lithium battery through a TP4056 module. The battery powers the ESP32, all four sensors, OLED display, and mixing motor — even at night and on cloudy days.
What sensors does BioBot have? +
BioBot uses a quad-sensor system — DHT22 for temperature and humidity (two readings from one sensor), MQ-135 for ammonia and CO₂ gas detection, a capacitive soil moisture probe for pile wetness, and an LDR light sensor for lid open/close detection. All readings update every 30 seconds.
How does Blynk monitoring work? +
The ESP32 sends all sensor data to Blynk's cloud every 30 seconds over WiFi. You can view live readings on the free Blynk app on Android or iPhone as gauges, graphs, and status labels. When any sensor goes out of range, Blynk sends an instant push notification with what to do.
How long does composting take? +
A full cycle takes 45 to 60 days through four stages: Mesophilic (Days 0–7), Thermophilic (Days 7–30), Cooling (Days 30–45), and Curing (Days 45–60). BioBot's DS3231 RTC tracks exact days and Blynk notifies you when compost is ready to harvest.
How does the auto-mixing motor work? +
A DC gear motor drives a horizontal paddle inside the perforated basket every 8 hours — running 5 seconds forward, a brief pause, then 5 seconds backward for thorough bidirectional aeration. You can also trigger manual mixing instantly via the Blynk app button.
How does WiFi setup work? +
BioBot uses WiFiManager. On first power-on, it creates a hotspot called "BioBot-Setup". Connect your phone to it, open 192.168.4.1 in your browser, select your home WiFi and enter the password. BioBot saves it and connects automatically from then on — no code changes needed.
What is the Gemini AI chatbot and how is it different from Blynk? +
Blynk shows live sensor data and sends hardware alerts. The Gemini AI chatbot on Telegram is completely independent — it answers any composting question intelligently, gives advice on what to add, fixing problems, and explains composting stages. Both work together but are separate systems.
Can BioBot work without WiFi? +
Yes. Without WiFi, BioBot continues reading all sensors, auto-mixing every 8 hours, counting days accurately via DS3231 RTC, and displaying live readings on the OLED screen. Only Blynk monitoring and the Telegram AI chatbot are unavailable without an internet connection.
